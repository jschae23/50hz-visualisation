50Hz

Fifty complete cycles every second, so one cycle lasts exactly 20 milliseconds. That is the rhythm of mains electricity across Europe, Asia, Africa and much of South America. Slow it down to watch a circle draw the wave.

The point goes once around the circle every 20 ms. The dashed line carries its height across, and the wave unrolls to the right, newest value on the left. Sound needs headphones or a subwoofer, since most laptop and phone speakers can’t reproduce 50 Hz.
